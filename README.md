# Study Habits in the AI Era

A research and data-analysis project exploring how Artificial Intelligence is influencing students' study habits, learning preferences, academic productivity, and approach to education.

This project was completed as the **final project of the Azisly Impact Sprint – 15-Day AI Live Project**, powered by Azisly.ai.

## 🎯 Project Overview

The project focuses on understanding the changing relationship between students and AI-powered learning tools. Through survey-based research, data analysis, and strategic reporting, it explores how students incorporate AI into their everyday learning routines.

The research aims to understand:
- How students use AI tools for learning and academic tasks.
- The influence of AI on traditional study habits.
- The perceived benefits of AI-assisted learning.
- Challenges and concerns associated with AI dependency.
- Students' perspectives on the future of AI in education.

## 📊 Research & Data Analysis

- Conducted a survey involving 56 participants.
- Collected responses about students' study habits and AI usage.
- Organized and analyzed survey data.
- Created an Excel-based analytical dashboard.
- Developed research findings and strategic insights.
- Presented the findings through a structured presentation.
